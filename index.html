The route
Seven nights,
three landscapes.

This is not a whistle-stop tour. Each location is chosen for atmosphere and access, with enough time to actually settle in.

1
Arusha
Arrival & first light
Fly into Kilimanjaro International Airport. Transfer to a quiet boutique inn on the Arusha outskirts — away from the town, with views of Mt. Meru. A gentle evening before the landscape begins.
2
Ngorongoro
The highland drive & crater rim
Three hours through highland forest. Arrive at the crater rim by mid-afternoon. Descend into the caldera at first light — 6:30 AM — before the convoys arrive. Spend the morning on the crater floor, then ascend for lunch and a quiet afternoon.
3
Ndutu / Southern Serengeti
Into the green plains
Transfer to Ndutu — a small, ten-tent camp on the edge of the conservation area. December is calving season: the plains are flush, the light is golden, and there are almost no other vehicles. One night here is never enough.
4–5
Eastern Serengeti
Two full days in the Serengeti
Move deeper into the Serengeti. Two nights at a seasonal tented camp near Seronera. Pre-dawn drives, long breakfasts, afternoon rest. December brings elephant herds to the woodlands and lion cubs to the kopjes. No rush, no checklist.
6
Seronera → Kilimanjaro
One last drive out
A pre-dawn game drive before the midday transfer to KIA — through acacia woodland and the long grass of the Seronera valley. Evening flight home, or onward.
